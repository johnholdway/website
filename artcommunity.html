ART COMMUNITY

Art community is more important that artists or art objects.

  • Reject the idea of individual artists as celebritis or the need for celebrities at all.
  • Embrase your local art community. Participate!
  • Produce and share art to build connections and foster collaboration.

Form Your Own Art Trading Events

I'm into small art pieces that I can collect and trade with other artists. I like Artist Trading Cards (ATCs). Postcard art is great too. I have been making them for years. The Discord is a place to share your events!
